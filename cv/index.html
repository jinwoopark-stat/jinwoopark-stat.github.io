Experience

  • 2024 - 2026

    Seoul, Korea

    Undergraduate Researcher
    Nonparametric Inference Lab, Department of Statistics, Seoul National University
    Advisor: Byeong U. Park
    • Derived uniform convergence rates for multivariate locally linear estimators under strong mixing, with an error expansion that separates stochastic variation, smoothing bias, and a term induced by local stationarity.
    • Proposed bias-corrected transfer learning estimators that connect a sparsely observed target series to densely observed auxiliary domains through a smoothly varying bias function over rescaled time and covariates.
    • Characterized a compatibility threshold, set by cross-domain curvature, separating the regime where transfer improves risk from the regime where heterogeneity leaves irreducible bias.
    • Validated the theory through simulation studies and an empirical analysis of international fuel prices.
    • Separately, built a multi-agent AutoGen pipeline for automated feature construction and variable selection in an industry collaboration (through Jan 2026).
