## Peer Tutoring, Seoul National University

I tutored for the Department of Statistics peer tutoring program for three semesters — Spring 2024, Fall 2024, and Spring 2025 — running weekly sessions of 26+ contact hours per semester in regression analysis, statistics, and probability. I was named an **Outstanding Tutor** twice, in Fall 2024 and Spring 2025.

What I took from it: the hard part of teaching statistics is almost never the algebra. It is finding the one sentence that turns a formula into something a student can picture. I still write my research notes that way.
